OUR COMMITMENT

Clear processes.
Responsible outcomes.

GNGC aims to conduct business in accordance with applicable laws, sanctions, export controls, customs requirements, product standards and environmental obligations in every relevant jurisdiction. Requirements vary by product, destination, transaction and project; professional review is part of our approach.

India export framework

Transactions are assessed against applicable DGFT/Foreign Trade Policy requirements, customs procedures, IEC and product-specific permits or restrictions. Documentation and classification are verified before shipment.

Destination-country rules

Local import licensing, customs valuation, taxes, standards, registration, local-content and installation rules are checked with qualified in-country advisers and importers.

Quality & certification

Product conformity and certifications are matched to applicable standards and tender requirements. ISO/IEC standards may be relevant; certification is never assumed without evidence.

Environment & safety

Project planning considers environmental assessment, waste and battery handling, worker safety, site requirements and end-of-life responsibilities as applicable.

Ethical procurement

We support transparent tendering, conflict-of-interest controls, anti-bribery practices, beneficial-owner checks and documented approval processes.

Sanctions & risk screening

Parties, banks, vessels, goods and end uses are screened against applicable sanctions and restricted-party requirements before commitments or shipment.

BILATERAL & REGIONAL CONTEXT

Market-specific diligence matters.

IN ↔ NG

India–Nigeria trade

Confirm current trade policy, import permits, standards approvals, customs documentation, FX/payment rules and procurement conditions for each transaction. Do not assume a preferential tariff or blanket bilateral exemption.

IN ↔ SD

India–Sudan trade

Conduct enhanced sanctions, banking, counterparty and logistics checks. Verify current restrictions and authorizations with competent authorities and specialist counsel before any Sudan-related transaction.

IN / ME

India & Middle East

Review destination-specific conformity, customs, free-zone/onshore rules, distributor obligations, VAT and tender conditions for each jurisdiction.

Important: This page describes a compliance approach, not legal advice or a claim of certification, government approval, bilateral treaty benefit or operational authorization. Rules change; obtain transaction-specific advice and official confirmation.
